### General feedback

**Working environment.** Dividing the project into pipeline, AWS, interface, accounts and administration helped me identify where teams needed to coordinate.

**Relevance to my studies.** The project gave me a chance to apply databases, SQL, data processing and cloud computing in one system.

**Learning opportunities.** I learned to trace metrics from collected data to the interface and investigate discrepancies with source tables.

**Teamwork.** The team agreed on fields, visibility rules and cutoff dates before reconciling figures. Members supported each other throughout the project.
